## Pilot Episode 1 — Production Brief

### Identity
- **Working title:** Nimo dan Cahaya Kecil yang Bergerak
- **Guardian:** Nimo
- **Duration:** 30 seconds
- **Primary philosophy:** Curiosity
- **Target:** PAUD/TK through early elementary, using language accessible to young children

### Learning Core
- **Learning outcome:** Children are encouraged to observe more closely and ask one question about something they see.
- **Development domain:** Cognitive and language.
- **Future skill:** Curiosity.
- **Character value:** Brave to try safely.
- **Wonder:** A small light moves and leaves a trail toward a portal.
- **Kindness action:** Nimo does not take or damage the light; he observes gently and invites friends.

### Child and Parent Extension
- **Quest:** Find one object that glows or reflects light with a parent and describe what is observed.
- **Parent question:** “Apa yang membuatmu ingin melihatnya lebih dekat?”
- **Parent activity:** Explore reflected light using safe objects at home with supervision.
- **Portal connection:** First observation quest on the website and initial Guardian Passport activation.
- **Suggested reward:** Badge “Mata Penjelajah”.

### Silent CSR Check
The quest can be completed without purchasing special tools. Alternatives without devices or glowing objects are provided through simple observation of color, shadow, or reflection. Success is measured by willingness to observe and ask questions, not speed or a single “correct” answer.
