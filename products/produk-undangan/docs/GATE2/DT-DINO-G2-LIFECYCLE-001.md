# DT-DINO-G2-LIFECYCLE-001 — Event-to-Memory Access Policy
Version 0.1 | FOUNDER REVIEWED & LOCKED — POLICY DIRECTION | 2026-10-13

## Founder-approved principles
1. Digital invitations, motion animation, 3D/WebAR and optional STEAM experiences do not inherently have to expire; hosted access has a defined service term.
2. Adopt **Event -> Memory** lifecycle: before event, full invitation functionality; event to day +90, Memory Mode with playable approved content and RSVP closed; after day +90, hosted interactive access may end or be extended according to published plan.
3. Standard proposal baseline: 90 days **after the event date** for hosted interactive experience. Event timezone and exact cut-off time must be defined in product terms and code.
4. Where licensed and supported, provide downloadable keepsake files (e.g., motion greeting, digital card) so memories are not dependent on perpetual hosting. Offline Holo video is preferred; physical Pepper's Ghost device still requires compatibility and optical/safety QC.
5. Preserve non-personal canonical Dino IP assets under internal asset lifecycle; never equate asset retention with retention of guests' personal information.
6. Explain access expiry, downloadable assets, renewal conditions and limitations clearly before purchase. No silent deletion of purchased deliverables.
7. Event/RSVP/guest personal data retention and deletion follow a **separate privacy schedule**, subject to explicit policy and applicable law; no indefinite guest-data storage by default.

## Explicitly not locked
Per-tier 90-day differences, free vs paid renewal, exact retention/deletion days, file formats and licenses, offline/WebAR feature availability, storage architecture, price and legal wording. All REVIEW REQUIRED.

## Required tests before release
Time-zone boundaries and daylight saving; RSVP cutoff; expired-link fallback; downloadable keepsake usability; reminder before expiry; deletion/export request; token revocation; browser offline behavior; backup/recovery and server cost model. NOT TESTED.
