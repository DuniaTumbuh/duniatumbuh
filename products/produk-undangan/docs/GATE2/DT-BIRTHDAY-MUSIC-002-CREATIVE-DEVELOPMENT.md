## Objective

## Working title
**Hari Penuh Warna / A Colorful Day** (working title only, trademark/title clearance not performed).

### Indonesian candidate — v0.1
Hari ceria, penuh warna
Kita berkumpul, tertawa bersama
Satu harapan, sejuta cerita
Ayo rayakan hari bahagia!

### English candidate — v0.1
Colors shining, bright today
Friends together, laugh and play
Make a wish and dream away
Let's celebrate your special day!

## Creative brief
- 110–120 BPM candidate, upbeat but gentle, 4/4, 30–45 seconds target.
- Suggested instruments: ukulele or gentle acoustic guitar, glockenspiel, soft piano and light percussion; original short Dino celebration sonic motif.
- Four deliverable families: Indonesian vocal, English vocal, instrumental, isolated celebration SFX (candle/confetti/applause). Avoid startling volume, clipping or forced playback.
- Name personalization through a separate optional, rights-cleared Dino greeting, not custom rendering of a new song for each event.
- Distinct Dino character voice requires canonical IP approval. Nimo & Friends separate IP and sonic identity.
- Both languages require prosody/meter adaptation to the same original melody; current drafts are not metrically aligned or singability-tested.
- Use the same licensed composition across many event configurations with versioned reusable media assets; no paid AI generation per guest visit.

## Creative QC and originality
1. Founder selects/revises lyrics and thematic identity.
2. Compose original melody; document composers, lyricists, arrangement and source project files.
3. Human review for similarity to existing works and for child-friendly wording; review Indonesian/English pronunciation and singability.
4. Produce demo only after separate production/budget authorization.
5. Clear composition, lyrics, masters, voice, performers, tools/samples, adaptation, commercial online embedding/synchronization, sublicensing, territories and duration with qualified review.
6. Record accessibility, audio loudness, mobile browser and opt-in playback test evidence.
7. Final Founder approval and immutable asset register before any product inclusion.
