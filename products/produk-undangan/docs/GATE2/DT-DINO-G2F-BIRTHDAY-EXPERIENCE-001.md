## Founder direction
Proceed with Gate 2F infrastructure decision and controlled implementation preparation. Add **interactive digital birthday cake** and **birthday music/SFX** to Motion Adventure's approved design scope, without blocking RSVP or essential event details.

## Birthday cake experience
- Original Dino-themed digital cake, age-appropriate styling; candle count configurable with sane upper bound, not necessarily tied to child's actual age.
- Candles glow, optional press-to-blow action (NO microphone permission by default), confetti and canonical Dino greeting.
- Reduced-motion, no-animation and low-bandwidth fallback; no flashing or photosensitive effects; no collection of child voice/face.
- Guest may skip entirely; celebration interaction must not gate address, RSVP or map.
- Preview supports mobile and muted default; event host may configure visibility.
- Character artwork must use separately approved canonical Dino IP; reference illustrations are not approved character assets.

## Music and audio
- Original or fully licensed reusable instrumental birthday track, optional SFX and Dino voice with verified rights for commercial use and distribution.
- No forced autoplay or sound-on by default; play only after user action, persistent visible mute and pause controls within session, reasonable default volume, respect reduced motion and user preferences.
- Avoid copyrighted popular song arrangements/recordings unless rights cleared; verify composition, recording, performance and synchronization rights.
- Pre-render/cache/version audio; no paid AI/TTS per guest visit. Separate voice generation consent/rights review if used.
- Audio quality gate: intelligibility, loudness, clipping, mobile browsers, accessibility and captions/text alternatives.

## Tier placement proposal — not final contract
Lite: static digital cake and optional short reusable celebratory sound (if chosen); Adventure: interactive cake, one canonical birthday track and approved Dino greeting; Premium: expanded cake themes and licensed track choices. Exact entitlements, customization, support and costs require Founder approval.
