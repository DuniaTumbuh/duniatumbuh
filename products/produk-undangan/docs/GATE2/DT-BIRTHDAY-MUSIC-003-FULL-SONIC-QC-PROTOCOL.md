## Founder question
Evaluate the combined lyrics, sung melody, vocal expression, arrangement and mix; determine whether the finished song sounds coherent, excessively Western, culturally comfortable for Indonesian and wider Asian family audiences, and appropriate for Dino Adventure.

## Current evidence

The synthetic tones cannot establish vocal prosody, final arrangement quality, commercial rights, or audience cultural acceptance. No blind listening panel or actual family/child testing performed.

## Comparative candidates
A: Western kids-pop reference direction — ukulele/acoustic guitar, handclaps, glockenspiel, major-key hook.
B: Indonesian–Asian warmth — natural Indonesian vocal prosody, restrained local-inspired rhythmic/timbral accents with cultural specificity reviewed by musicians; avoid tokenistic or stereotyped use of traditional instruments.
C: Universal hybrid (recommended testing lead) — accessible contemporary acoustic-pop core, Indonesian-first vocal phrasing and tasteful regionally grounded accents, separate English adaptation.
All versions should use the SAME original melody and identical lyrics for fair arrangement comparison, or disclose differences.

## Blind QC matrix (100 points, proposed)
- Vocal singability and ID/EN prosody: 20
- Child friendliness and emotional warmth: 15
- Originality/distinct sonic identity: 15
- Indonesian cultural fit: 15
- Cross-cultural accessibility (Asia/global): 10
- Arrangement, instrument balance and mix: 10
- Catchiness/memorability without overstimulation: 10
- Accessibility and control (no autoplay, mute/pause, safe loudness): 5

Suggested pilot sample: Indonesian parents and educators plus age-appropriate child feedback with parental consent; include listeners familiar with English. Blind order rotation, volume normalization, no collecting children's personal data. Record comments and ratings separately for ID and EN. This is a proposed method, not a validated market-research result.

## Mandatory fail-closed gates
- No final claim of musical PASS before complete ID and EN vocal renders with arrangements, loudness-normalized and auditioned on phone, tablet and speakers/headphones.
- No copyright clearance based on stylistic impressions; composition, lyrics, master, performers, samples, AI tool and commercial audiovisual distribution rights independently documented.
- No publication/paid production without Founder approval and rights review. Current PR #11 DRAFT, no deployment.
