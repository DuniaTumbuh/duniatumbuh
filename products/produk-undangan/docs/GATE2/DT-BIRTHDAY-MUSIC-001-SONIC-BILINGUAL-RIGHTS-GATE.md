## Approved design
Create a reusable original birthday-song family for Dunia Tumbuh invitation products: Indonesian vocal, English vocal, one language-neutral instrumental, and modular celebration SFX. Both vocal versions may share an original composition with separately reviewed original lyrics. Dino greetings must use approved canonical character voice; Nimo & Friends remain a separate IP universe and must not inherit Dino identity or voice automatically.
Music optional; play only after user interaction; mute/pause/volume controls, text alternatives, and quiet default. RSVP and essential event details remain available without audio. No AI generation on each guest view; use cached versioned, rights-cleared assets.

## Rights clearance (fail closed)
Before importing or distributing any music: identify and document composition, lyrics in each language, arrangement, sound recording/master, performance and voice rights, commissioned-work agreements, third-party samples, and AI tool terms. Verify rights for commercial invitations, synchronization/embedding with visuals, online streaming/download where applicable, reproduction, adaptation/translation, sublicensing to platform customers as necessary, territories, term, and use across many events. Confirm moral-rights/attribution obligations, credits, withdrawal and takedown handling, and provenance evidence. Do not assume a song is globally public-domain because it is public-domain in the United States; do not assume an existing recording, cover, arrangement, translation, YouTube/Spotify stream, stock-audio label or AI output is cleared for commercial redistribution. No popular commercial song is approved by this policy.

## Proposed creative and QA brief
- Candidate original melody and lyric hooks in ID and EN, human-reviewed for originality, pronunciation, inclusivity and suitability for children.
- Instrumental and SFX separated into stems; no excessive loudness, clipping or startling transient effects; check accessibility and user control.
- Document asset ID, version, source, author/performer, license proof, allowed uses, restrictions, expiration, checksum, approval, and fallback.
- Pre-publication human similarity and rights review, followed by audio QC and browser/mobile playback QC.
- No lyrics or recorded audio have yet been commissioned, created or accepted in this gate.
