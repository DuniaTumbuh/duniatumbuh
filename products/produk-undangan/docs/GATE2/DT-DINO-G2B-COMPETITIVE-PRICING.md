# DT-DINO-G2B-002 — Competitive Positioning and Pilot Pricing
Date 2026-10-13 | Status: PROPOSAL / MARKET VALIDATION REQUIRED

## Evidence and comparability
Paperless Post offers free sending for first 50 direct recipients and free shareable links for free cards; paid feature sets priced per guest, RSVP and broadcasts included in multiple tiers. See https://www.paperlesspost.com/pricing and https://paperlesspost.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/206187576-Is-Paperless-Post-free-Can-I-send-for-free .
Indonesian market vendor-authored guides indicate wide ranges, e.g. Menica suggests animated premium invitations around Rp100k-350k and Invitasiku cites an around Rp79k silver tier. These are marketing content, NOT a representative independent survey, and often wedding rather than children's invitations: https://menica.pro/blog/harga-undangan-digital and https://invitasiku.id/blog/harga-undangan-digital .

## Proposed test prices — not validated market prices
Lite: Rp49,000/event — responsive Dino template, event details, QR/link, countdown, basic RSVP, no paid AI generation.
Adventure: Rp99,000/event — Lite + shared Dino motion, canonical greeting, 1 STEAM activity, RSVP dashboard, approved event personalization; 1 bounded generation job if offered.
Premium: Rp179,000/event — Adventure + expanded themes, up to 3 bounded AI generation jobs, parent-friendly guest update tools and premium support scope.
Physical Pop-Up and Holo are separate add-ons pending BOM, vendor quotations, child-safety and optical QC. Do not promise 3D/WebAR/Holo in tiers until device/physical tests PASS.

## Differentiation hypothesis
Against conventional static invites: age-appropriate optional STEAM play, canonical original Dino IP, 3D/motion fallback, organizer revision/publish without redeploy, centralized RSVP, secure per-guest access. Against sophisticated event platforms, do not claim superior guest management without measured feature-by-feature benchmark.

## Sales and cost controls
Prices include clearly specified active period, guest limit and support/revision allowance (all to be defined and tested); no unlimited AI promises. Customer owns their event details; define export/deletion/retention. Pilot price A/B testing with real conversion and support costs; avoid claiming profit until unit economics measured.

## Proposed acceptance gate
At least 3 direct competitor SKU checks in Indonesia with same guest count, animation, RSVP and customization scope; 10 parent interviews or pilot customers; load/performance measurements; per-order COGS ledger; founder approval of price/term/guarantees.
