## Founder verdict
Candidate-04 sounds unclear, messy and unsuitable. The song/music must create a positive psychological experience for children and parents. Do not promote C04 or iterate it by adding more layers or tempo.

## Root-cause hypotheses (not verified by blind listening or technical DAW inspection)
- Simultaneous tempo changes and denser beat programming reduced perceived rhythmic stability.
- Added instrumental layers competed with the recognizable C03 signature hook.
- No vocal-led phrase design or emotionally shaped harmony; synthetic demo cannot validate family-audience response.
These are engineering/creative hypotheses, not measured acoustic diagnoses.

## Remediation direction
- Return to C03 as a *preferred creative reference*, not a cleared master.
- Keep a stable pulse (~116–124 BPM to test); create energy through musical phrasing, contrast, selective instrumentation and dynamics, not a continuous accelerando.
- 0–3s: clean recognizable sonic hook; 3–12s: uncluttered sung melody; 12–22s: warm lift with one added rhythmic or harmonic element at a time; 22–28s: brief emotional pause for wish/candle; 28–36s: joyful full-but-controlled refrain and signature ending.
- Lead melody always intelligible; use simple singable intervals and repeated motifs, coherent chord changes, no overlapping hooks, no startling transients.
- ID and EN vocal prosody checked separately; warm family tone and age-appropriate volume, opt-in playback.
- Compare two controlled treatments: A gentle cinematic-family and B universal upbeat acoustic, SAME melody/lyrics/tempo; no imitation of major studios.
- Evaluate children: unprompted 2–3s recognition (after familiarization), singability, joyful engagement, tolerability on repetition. Evaluate parents: warmth, trust, emotional fit and lack of irritation. Avoid claiming universal psychological effects.
- Qualified music producer and family listening panel, with informed parent consent and no child PII retained.
- Independent similarity and commercial music/voice rights clearance before publication.

## Required evidence before advancing
Complete 30–45-second sung ID and EN arrangements, reference mix and stems, controlled loudness, blind scorecards, device playback results, rights ledger, Founder sign-off.
