## Baseline product rules
1. Core event info, address, map handoff, and RSVP always reachable without game, AR, sound, account creation by guest, or AI.
2. All tiers use secure organizer authentication, event-level authorization, server-side validation, rate limiting, token revocation, consent notice, accessible mobile layout, and privacy-aware analytics. Security is never a paid upgrade.
3. No child account required; no face/voice biometric collection for personalization by default. No publicly indexed private guest data.
4. Respect prefers-reduced-motion, mute/autoplay browser restrictions, slow devices, limited bandwidth, and fallback to accessible static HTML.
5. Event-specific assets have approved rights, versioned revisions, download terms and expiration notice. No indefinite hosted-service promise.
6. Every package must have a measurable variable cost envelope and per-event ledger. No generative AI on guest page load.

## Product variants
Pop-Up Explorer: physical invitation + QR linking to same event record; physical BOM, shipping, child-safe materials and QR durability pending.
Motion Adventure: digital animated invitation; first MVP candidate, subject to accessibility and device QC.
Holo Adventure: Pepper's Ghost physical viewer with local compatible video; NOT true hologram; optical angle, luminance, safety and manufacturing tests NOT RUN.
3D/WebAR: optional progressive enhancement; device/browser and fallback tests NOT RUN.
Nimo & Friends: separate future universe, not silently included in Dino licenses or art assets.
