# DT-AR-BETA-001 — Educational Content v1.0

**Status:** QC CANDIDATE — NOT CANONICAL

- **EDU-01 — Benih Kehidupan:** seed, root, shoot, sprout, plant.
- **EDU-02 — Apa yang Tanaman Butuhkan?:** water, sunlight, air, soil/nutrients.
- **EDU-03 — Pohon dan Lingkungan:** age-appropriate photosynthesis, oxygen and carbon dioxide.
- **EDU-04 — Akar Menjaga Tanah:** roots/vegetation, soil stability, erosion/runoff and rain.
- **EDU-05 — Kita Menjaga Hutan:** caring for plants, avoiding destructive behavior, forest-fire/cutting awareness, small positive actions.

“Lungs of the Earth” may only be used as a familiar metaphor with careful explanation; it shall not replace the underlying science. Silent CSR values are integrated naturally through care, empathy and shared responsibility.
