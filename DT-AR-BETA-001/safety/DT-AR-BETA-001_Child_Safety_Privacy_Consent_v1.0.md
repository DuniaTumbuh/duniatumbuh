# DT-AR-BETA-001 — Child Safety, Privacy and Consent v1.0

**Status:** CANONICAL — FOUNDER APPROVED

## P0 Controls
No facial recognition, biometric processing, unsupervised autonomous AI identification, persistent child accounts, or unnecessary recording/storage.

## Consent / Distribution Classes
- **A — Live classroom participation**
- **B — Internal documentation**
- **C — School/family distribution**
- **D — Public/social/marketing publication**

Authorization in one class does not automatically authorize another class. Documentation permission shall not be interpreted as public-release permission.

Local-first photo capture is preferred. Public gallery creation is outside Beta scope. Any public documentary derivative requires privacy/consent QC before release.
